## Recommendations

1. **JavaFX 3D**
   - **Best for**: Beginners, simple 3D applications, and educational projects
   - **Strengths**: 
     - Excellent Eclipse integration
     - Low learning curve
     - Good documentation and community support
   - **Limitations**: 
     - Limited advanced 3D rendering capabilities
     - Lack of built-in physics engine
   - **Recommendation**: Ideal for creating simple interactive 3D puzzles like a basic Rubik's cube simulation, especially in educational settings or for rapid prototyping.

2. **JOGL (Java OpenGL)**
   - **Best for**: Advanced simulations and high-performance applications
   - **Strengths**: 
     - Superior 3D rendering capabilities
     - Fine-grained control over graphics pipeline
     - Excellent for complex mathematical simulations
   - **Limitations**: 
     - Steeper learning curve
     - More complex Eclipse integration
   - **Recommendation**: Suitable for developers who need precise control over rendering and are implementing complex 3D puzzles with advanced graphics requirements.

3. **jMonkeyEngine (JME)**
   - **Best for**: Comprehensive game development and complex simulations
   - **Strengths**: 
     - Built-in physics engine (Bullet Physics)
     - Advanced 3D rendering capabilities
     - Strong documentation and community support
     - Good balance of features and ease of use
   - **Limitations**: 
     - Moderate learning curve
     - Requires additional setup for Eclipse integration
   - **Recommendation**: The most versatile choice for creating sophisticated 3D puzzle simulations, offering a good balance between ease of use and powerful capabilities.

## Framework Selection Guide

1. **For Simple 3D Puzzles and Educational Projects**:
   - **Recommended**: JavaFX 3D
   - **Rationale**: JavaFX 3D offers the easiest integration with Eclipse and has a gentle learning curve, making it ideal for educational settings and simple 3D puzzle implementations like a basic Rubik's cube.

2. **For High-Performance and Complex Simulations**:
   - **Recommended**: JOGL
   - **Rationale**: JOGL provides direct access to OpenGL functions, allowing for fine-tuned control over rendering. This makes it suitable for complex 3D puzzles that require high performance or custom rendering techniques.

3. **For Comprehensive 3D Puzzle Development**:
   - **Recommended**: jMonkeyEngine
   - **Rationale**: jMonkeyEngine offers a robust set of tools, including a physics engine and advanced 3D capabilities. It's well-suited for creating feature-rich, interactive 3D puzzles with realistic physics simulations.
